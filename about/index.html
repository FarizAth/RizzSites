About

A studio built around one thing.

RizzSites designs and builds websites. That's the whole business, and we like it that way.

Why RizzSites exists

Most small business websites look like they were built in an afternoon, because most of them were. RizzSites exists to fix that: real design thinking and solid engineering behind every site we ship, without the price tag of a full agency.

We work across a handful of industries we understand well, HVAC, plumbing, construction, auto, fitness, organizing, and restaurants, plus the occasional business outside those lanes. Every project on the Work page is a real site we built.

How we work

One project at a time, start to finish. We handle design and development ourselves, so nothing gets lost between a designer and a developer who've never talked to each other.

We're not a marketing agency and we won't pretend to be one. We build the website. If you need ads, social, or ongoing SEO campaigns, we'll be upfront that it's outside what we do.

What We Believe

A few things we won't compromise on.

Fast beats flashy

A beautiful site that takes 8 seconds to load loses to a good-looking site that loads instantly, every time.

Real content over filler

No lorem ipsum left behind and no stock-photo sameness if we can help it. Your site should look like your business.

Built to last

Clean code that you, or whoever comes after us, can actually work with later.
